# 🏨📱 Shadify — Destination Hospitality Concierge & Future Roadmap

> Solving the chaos of destination wedding room assignments, airport pickups, and family helpdesk contacts, plus the long-term native mobile app vision.

---

## 1. Destination Resort Room & Transport Concierge

When a guest visits the website or opens their personal link, the site renders an interactive **"Stay & Travel Pass"**:

### 1. Accommodation Card
- Hotel/Resort Name, Address, and 1-tap Google Maps link.
- Allocated Wing, Floor, and Room Number(s) (e.g. *"Lake Wing, Room #304 & #305"*).
- Check-in & Check-out dates and timings.
- Resort Wi-Fi network and password.
- Key collection desk location (e.g. *"Lobby Helpdesk #2"*).

### 2. Transport & Cab Details
- Vehicle Model (e.g. *"White Toyota Innova Crysta"*).
- License Plate Number (e.g. *"RJ 27 TA 4421"*).
- Driver Name and direct `[ 📞 Call ]` and `[ 💬 WhatsApp ]` buttons.
- Airport/Station meeting point (e.g. *"Exit Gate #2, Arrival Terminal"*).

### 3. Emergency & Hospitality Leads
- Dedicated family coordinators with 1-tap call buttons:
  - Transport Coordinator (Brother/Cousin name & phone).
  - Room & Luggage Lead (Sister/Cousin name & phone).
  - Resort Duty Manager extension.
  - Doctor on call & nearest hospital details.

## 2. Future Native Mobile App Strategy (React Native / Expo)

- **Phase 1–3**: PWA and mobile web first. 95% of guests access seamlessly via WhatsApp without downloading an app.
- **Phase 4 (Expansion - Ultra-Luxury Bespoke Plan: ₹34,999+)**:
  - Standalone branded iOS & Android app on the App Store (e.g., *"Priya & Rahul Wedding"*).
  - Couple's custom royal monogram as the app icon.
  - Home screen live countdown widgets.
  - Offline photo viewing on flights for relatives.
