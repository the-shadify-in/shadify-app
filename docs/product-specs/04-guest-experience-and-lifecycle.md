## 2. Interactive Feature Highlights

### 1. CSS / Lottie Royal Envelope Reveal

- Fast, battery-friendly, and instant-loading on venue 4G/5G connections (zero Three.js/WebGL bloat).
- A royal envelope with gold borders and a royal seal bearing the couple's custom monogram.
- Guest taps the seal ➡️ Envelope unfolds smoothly with a soft paper sound/haptic feedback, presenting the wedding invitation card.

### 2. 30-Second Personal Video Bite

- A vertical selfie video recorded on the couple's phone:
  _"Namaste! We are so excited to invite you to celebrate with us in Udaipur. Tap below to see our schedule and RSVP!"_
- Auto-plays muted with elegant animated subtitles. Hosted directly on Cloudflare R2.

### 3. Digital Shagun Envelope (Direct UPI QR)

- Presets: `₹501`, `₹1,101`, `₹2,101`, `₹5,101`, `₹11,001` or Custom.
- Direct 1-tap UPI deep link (`upi://pay?pa=...`) straight into the couple's bank account with **₹0 commission**.
- No payment aggregator webhooks needed; guest enters their name and transaction note, and the couple confirms it manually in the admin ledger upon bank receipt.

### 4. "Meet the VIPs" & Wedding Who's Who

- Visual cards celebrating the core wedding party:
  - **The Pillars**: Parents of the Bride & Groom.
  - **The Bride's Tribe**: Bridesmaids & sisters with playful bios (_"Chief Shoe Negotiators 👠"_).
  - **The Groom's Squad**: Best men & brothers (_"Baraat Hype Men"_).
  - **Emergency Coordinators**: Family members managing logistics, stay, and transport with phone numbers.
  - **Celebrity Performers & Live Bands**: Featured artists at the Sangeet with music preview links.
  - **The Officiating Priest (Pandit Ji)**: Guiding the sacred Vedic Saptapadi vows.
